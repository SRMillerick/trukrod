The Story

A quarter-century of wrench time

Twenty-six years ago, a 1969 GMC Stepside came home in pieces — and stayed that way for a long, beautiful while. What followed was decades of nights and weekends: metal shaped by hand, parts hunted down and rebuilt, standards raised again and again.

This isn’t a checkbook build. It’s a life’s work measured in shop hours, scarred knuckles, and the quiet stubbornness of never saying “good enough.”

TRUKROD is the name it earned. This site is the record — every chapter of the build, in order — and home base for the run to Las Vegas.

The Stakes

From the garage
to 1:64 scale

On Saturday, September 26, TRUKROD rolls into the Las Vegas stop of the Hot Wheels Legends Tour — where Mattel’s judges aren’t counting receipts. They’re engineers hunting for the one thing money can’t buy: a genuine garage build with Hot Wheels spirit. A blown stepside in Earnhardt red, riding on the widest rubber made, built over 26 years by one man? That’s not a pitch. That’s just how Rob builds.

If the truck makes the cut, 26 years of blood, sweat and steel become a 1:64 die-cast shipped around the world.
